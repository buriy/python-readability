Statute of Limitations Length for Personal Injury

Florida’s statute of limitations maintains that you have four years from the data of the incident to file a personal injury claim. The sooner you file, however, the better your odds are of obtaining compensation. Filing soon after the injury means that all the details will be fresh in your mind and in the minds of any eyewitnesses. There are three basic types of damages that you can receive compensation for: economic, non-economic, and punitive. Economic losses include anything that has a quantifiable monetary value associated with it, such as medical costs, property damages, loss of earnings, or the cost of services. Non-economic are more difficult to assign a numerical value and include pain and suffering, loss of life, or physical impairment. Punitive damages, although rare in personal injury cases, can be awarded in instances of gross negligence. If you believe you have suffered any of these damages at the hands of another, don’t wait.
